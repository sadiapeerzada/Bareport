Bareport

A zero-dependency security reconnaissance and attack-surface assessment tool for hosts and services on your network — built entirely on the Go standard library.

What Bareport Does

Network & Service Discovery
TCP/UDP port scanning, service banner grabbing, TTL-based OS fingerprinting, and local-subnet auto-discovery.
TLS & HTTP Auditing
Certificate validity, expiry, and self-signed checks; cipher/version inspection; HTTP security-header audits.
DNS Reconnaissance
MX/TXT/SPF/DMARC lookups and reverse DNS for every target, alongside the port scan.
Risk Scoring & Findings
Every result runs through a findings engine and rolls up into a single 0–100 risk score with remediation guidance.

Why Bareport

Zero Third-Party Dependencies

No third-party Go modules — not for scanning, TLS, HTTP, DNS, JSON, or the dashboard you're looking at right now. go.mod has zero require lines, and the claim is independently checkable: run bareport --verify-zero-dep or inspect the generated deps-proof.txt.

Quick Start

bareport --targets 127.0.0.1 --ports 80,443run a scan and print a report to the terminal
bareport --targets 127.0.0.1 --ports 80,443 --serverun a scan and launch this dashboard against it
bareport --targets 127.0.0.1 --watchre-scan on an interval and report drift over time
bareport --verify-zero-depprove the zero-dependency claim at runtime
